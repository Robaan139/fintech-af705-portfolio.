# fintech-af705-portfolio.

The notebook accomplishes three primary objectives:
Taxonomy & Classification: Constructs a structured dataset of 32 global FinTech companies and categorizes each into a standard 7-category taxonomy (Payments & Digital Wallets, Digital Banking & Neobanks, Lending Tech & BNPL, WealthTech & Robo-Advisory, InsurTech, Blockchain & Crypto, RegTech).
It includes academic justifications for nuanced edge cases (e.g., Klarna, Robinhood, Chainalysis). Pakistani Ecosystem Analysis: Builds a dedicated comparative mapping table evaluating major Pakistani FinTech platforms (NayaPay, SadaPay, JazzCash, Easypaisa, Mashreq Bank Pakistan, HugoBank, RAAST, Finja) against the taxonomy, highlighting regulatory and operational facts.Data Visualization: 
Generates visual distributions (bar chart) showing the breakdown of companies across the taxonomy categories.
